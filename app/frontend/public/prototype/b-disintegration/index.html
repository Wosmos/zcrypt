Why this exists

Free storage was never free.

01

I ran out of room

Drive gave me 15 GB. I made three accounts and shuffled files between them like a low-budget digital smuggler.

02

The free terabyte had a catch

TeraBox offered a terabyte for nothing. The nothing turned out to be my photos, indexed, and an app that would not let go.

03

Then it failed at 80 percent

A 4 GB upload, fifty minutes in, died at 80 percent. No resume. Start again. I closed the laptop instead.

04

Every free plan was paid for with the contents of my files.

The price tag was me.

So I built the one that cannot read them.
